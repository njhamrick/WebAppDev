About Me

Hello! My name is Nikki Hamrick. I am a veteran and served in the U.S. Navy for five years as a Hospital Corpsman. In my free time, I enjoy crocheting, reading, painting,and fishing. I have two cats, Poseidon and Opal, who keep me company while I crochet and read. I currently work the night shift at the Youth Crisis Center in Twin Falls. I spend most of my shift monitoring the facility and making sure everything stays safe and quiet throughout the night. Since the night shift is usually pretty quiet, I'm able to use that time to work on my schoolwork.
